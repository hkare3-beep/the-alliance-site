Research Pathway

Conduct meaningful research with faculty mentorship and peer collaboration.

What is Research at The Alliance?

Our research pathway brings together students passionate about investigating real-world questions across STEM, social science, design, and humanities. You'll work with faculty mentors and peer researchers to design, conduct, and present original research.

Current Research Areas

Environmental Science

Research on urban sustainability, climate resilience, and environmental justice in Chicago.

Social Science & Policy

Studies on community engagement, public health, and policy solutions for urban challenges.

Technology & Data

Data analysis, machine learning, and computational methods applied to social challenges.

How to Get Started

Attend an info session, express your research interests, and connect with faculty mentors. No prior research experience necessary—just curiosity and willingness to learn.
