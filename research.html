Research Pathway

SAIR’s research activities help students investigate questions that matter to them and understand the problems they want to address.

Learn Through Research

Members can ask questions, speak with people, read reliable sources, and learn basic research methods. Research is intended to be approachable for beginners and useful for students who already have experience.

From Questions to Understanding

Research gives project teams a stronger foundation for deciding what to build, who it should serve, and how to measure whether it is useful.
