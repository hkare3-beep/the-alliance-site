Join The Alliance

Start your journey as a researcher and innovator today.

We're always accepting new members!

There's no application process—just show up, meet the team, and get started. Here's how:

Step 1: Attend an Info Session

Our first info session is Tuesday, September 15 at 5:00 PM in the Campus Center. No RSVP needed—just show up!

Step 2: Choose Your Pathway

Decide if you want to join the Research pathway or the Projects pathway (or both!). Each meets once a week.

Step 3: Start Attending Meetings

Research: Tuesdays, 4-5:30 PM
Projects: Thursdays, 4-5:30 PM

Step 4: Find Your Project

Work with the team to find a research project or idea that matches your interests and skills.

What to Expect

  • ✓ Collaborative work environment with peers from all disciplines
  • ✓ Faculty mentorship and guidance
  • ✓ Skill-building workshops and training
  • ✓ Opportunities to present your work
  • ✓ Supportive community of ambitious students
  • ✓ Flexible commitment—work at your own pace

Questions?

Email us at alliance@uic.edu or find us on campus. We're here to help!
