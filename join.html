Join SAIR

Who Should Join?

SAIR is for students from every major who want to ask questions, learn new skills, meet collaborators, explore research, or help create something useful. No coding or research experience is required.

What Members Do

Members explore questions, do basic research, work on projects, learn from one another, and share their results. You can contribute through research, planning, building, analysis, communication, design, or outreach.

Bring Your Perspective

Students can join a project team or contribute skills that help make an idea clearer, more useful, and easier to share.
