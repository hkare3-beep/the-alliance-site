About The Alliance

Our mission, values, and the people building this together.

Our Mission

The Alliance is an interdisciplinary student organization dedicated to fostering collaborative research, innovation, and problem-solving at the University of Illinois Chicago. We believe that the best ideas come from bringing together students with different perspectives, skills, and experiences.

Our Values

Collaboration

We thrive when we work together across disciplines and backgrounds.

Rigor

We maintain high standards in our research, projects, and work.

Impact

We create solutions that matter beyond the classroom.

How It Works

Members join one of two pathways: Research or Projects. Each pathway includes regular meetings, skill-building workshops, mentorship from faculty and experienced students, and opportunities to present your work.
