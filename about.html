About SAIR

Why SAIR?

Many students want to try research or create something useful but do not know where to start. Students from different majors also need more opportunities to work together outside class. SAIR creates that space through student-led, inclusive, and interdisciplinary work.

Our Values

Student-led

Students shape the questions, projects, and experiences they want to pursue.

Inclusive

Everyone should have a welcoming place to ask questions, learn, and contribute.

Interdisciplinary

Different majors and perspectives make stronger questions and more useful outcomes.

Who Can Contribute?

Everyone can contribute. Engineering students might design or test a prototype; math and computing students might analyze information or build a tool; business students might help with planning and implementation; humanities and social science students might study people’s needs; and design or communication students might make the result easier to understand and use. No coding or research experience is required.

How Projects Develop

Idea → Research → Plan → Create → Test → Share

Projects should be small enough to complete in a realistic timeframe. Teams learn by testing their ideas, improving them, and explaining what worked, what did not, and what comes next.
