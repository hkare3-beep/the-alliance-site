Who We Are

Student Association for Innovation and Research.

SAIR brings students from different majors together to learn from one another, explore research questions, and turn ideas into useful projects. It is a welcoming place to ask questions, find teammates, try new skills, and create something meaningful.

🔬

Research

Explore questions, learn how to investigate them, and build confidence through hands-on research.

💡

Innovation

Turn ideas into practical proposals, prototypes, guides, events, and other useful outcomes.

🤝

Collaboration

Work across majors and perspectives to create results no one person could produce alone.
